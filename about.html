Our Story

Redefining Travel Since 2004

FIRST CAB was founded with a simple mission — to provide safe, reliable and comfortable transportation for everyone in Chennai. What started as a small fleet of 5 cars has grown into one of the city's most trusted cab services.

We believe every journey should be stress-free and enjoyable. That's why we maintain our vehicles to the highest standards and ensure our drivers are professionally trained and background-verified.

Today, we serve thousands of passengers every month — from early-morning airport runs to multi-day outstation trips — always with the same commitment to quality that started us back in 2004.
